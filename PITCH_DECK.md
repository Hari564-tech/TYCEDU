# SwarVidya — Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1 — SwarVidya
**Learn by speaking. Understand in your language.**

A voice-first learning companion for Indian school students.

Visual: student using a phone with a large microphone action; Telugu, Hindi and English language labels.

## Slide 2 — The problem
- Many students are more comfortable speaking their mother tongue than typing English.
- Text-heavy learning apps can create friction for students who read English slowly.
- Students may hesitate to ask doubts in a classroom.
- Teachers need a quick view of concepts students find difficult.

Speaker note: The problem is not a lack of curiosity. It is the interface between a learner and the learning resource.

## Slide 3 — Who we serve
- Students aged 10–16, especially Classes 6–10
- Government and low-resource schools
- First-generation learners and students sharing family devices
- Teachers who need topic-level learning signals

## Slide 4 — The solution
SwarVidya turns a spoken doubt into a simple explanation, spoken response and short practice activity.

Core loop: **Speak → Understand → Practise → Improve**

## Slide 5 — Product demo
1. Select class, subject and language.
2. Ask “What is photosynthesis?” by voice.
3. Hear a simple explanation in the selected language.
4. Answer a one-question quiz by voice or tap.
5. View topic-level progress in the teacher dashboard.

## Slide 6 — Why it is different
- Voice-first, not typing-first
- Mother-tongue-friendly experience
- Designed for small screens and simple navigation
- Short explanations followed by retrieval practice
- Teacher-facing insights, not just student content

## Slide 7 — Impact and measurement
Pilot metrics to validate:
- Doubts asked per learner per week
- Quiz completion and accuracy by topic
- Repeat practice rate
- Student confidence before/after a learning session
- Teacher-reported usefulness of weak-topic insights

Do not claim impact until a school pilot has measured it.

## Slide 8 — Launch plan
**Phase 1 — Prototype:** Validate the voice-to-practice loop with students and teachers.

**Phase 2 — School pilot:** Co-design Telugu and English content with teachers; test on low-cost Android phones.

**Phase 3 — Expand:** Add curriculum-aligned lessons, more Indian languages, teacher groups and privacy-conscious school reporting.

## Slide 9 — Trust and safety
- Age-appropriate, curriculum-reviewed explanations
- Teacher/school controls for classroom use
- Parent/guardian and school consent where required
- Data minimisation and clear retention rules
- Secure backend for any future AI integration

## Slide 10 — The ask
Partner with us to pilot SwarVidya in classrooms, test the learning loop and improve multilingual voice access.

**Closing:** A student should not need to type perfect English to ask a great question.
